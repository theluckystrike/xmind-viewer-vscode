Drag the map to move it. Ctrl or Cmd + wheel zooms at the mouse pointer. Press `F` to fit the sheet, `0` for 100%.

Click a topic to select it; the arrow keys move between topics. `Space` or a double-click collapses or expands a branch. The number on a collapsed branch is the count of hidden topics.

Levels shows only the first 1 to 4 levels. Expand all shows every topic again.
