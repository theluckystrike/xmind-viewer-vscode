# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First release: XMind 2020+, XMind Zen and XMind 8 files, FreeMind .mm and OPML.
- Every sheet as a tab. Relationships, boundaries, summaries, markers, labels, notes, pictures and LaTeX equations (KaTeX).
- Map, logic chart, tree and org chart layouts. Collapse, levels, search, zoom and pan, keyboard moves.
- Export to PNG, SVG and Markdown. Dark and light themes.
- Safe reading: zip bomb and path checks, nothing unpacked to disk, no network.
- Support links (Sponsor, More free viewers, Rate, Hide) and a one-time rating question you can turn off.
